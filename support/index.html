Common questions

Can I try it without an API key?
Yes. Select Demo in Settings.

Where is my API key stored?
In Apple Keychain on your device. The developer cannot see it.

Why does voice input not start?
Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in iOS Settings. Your language and device must support on-device recognition.

How do I erase a conversation?
Open Settings and choose Delete chat history.

Is this therapy?
No. Lantern Companion is fictional AI entertainment and is not an emergency or professional service.
