Lantern Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Lantern Companion has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics. Sessions, chat history, drafts, and response preferences are stored on your device and may be included in your device backups. Sessions are not synced by Lantern.

AI providers
If you choose OpenAI or a custom compatible provider, Settings identifies the recipient and asks you to turn on “Allow AI data sharing” before any connected chat request. When you send a message, up to 20 recent messages from the current session (including any speech transcript you send), your saved response preferences, selected character, model ID, and API key are sent directly to that provider to generate a reply. The provider also receives connection information such as your IP address. Other sessions and microphone recordings are not included. Changing the provider or custom endpoint requires permission again. Turn permission off in Settings to stop future requests; doing so cannot delete data already received by a provider. Demo mode sends no chat data over the network.

OpenAI requests go to api.openai.com. OpenAI's policy is available at https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/. Custom requests go to the HTTPS endpoint you enter; identify its operator and read its privacy policy before allowing sharing. The selected provider's retention practices and charges apply. Lantern requests that OpenAI not store the response object, but this does not guarantee zero provider retention. Do not submit information you do not want your chosen provider to receive.

Provider service logs
AI and download providers may retain service-use and diagnostic records, such as request times, model/download selections, IP addresses and device/OS information. IP addresses may reveal approximate location. Authenticated AI requests can be associated with your provider account. Providers use this information to operate, secure and analyze their services under their own policies. Lantern does not add an analytics SDK or send advertising identifiers. See OpenAI API data controls and Hugging Face privacy.

API keys
Keys are protected in Apple Keychain using device-only accessibility. Remove a saved key with “Remove saved key” in Settings. Uninstalling an app does not reliably remove its Keychain items.

Speech and audio
Microphone access begins only when you record a voice message. Recognition is required to run on device. The app does not retain microphone recordings. If you send the resulting transcript to a connected AI, it is treated as chat text. Spoken replies are generated locally with Apple speech or the optional Supertonic model.

Optional voice download
Supertonic files are downloaded directly from Hugging Face. Hugging Face and its delivery providers receive normal connection information, including your IP address. Model files are stored locally, excluded from backups, and removable in Settings. No chat text is sent to Hugging Face for voice generation. You can skip the download and use the basic voice.

Your controls
Reopen, rename, or delete individual sessions from the Sessions panel. Delete all conversation history, edit or clear response preferences, remove API keys, remove the voice pack, and choose your provider in Settings. Revoke microphone or speech permissions in iOS Settings. Requests to delete information already sent to a provider must be directed to that provider.

Public website
The support, tutorial and legal pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting these pages sends normal connection information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. These pages do not use analytics scripts, forms or advertising. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Age and contact
The app is intended for people aged 16 and older, not children.
